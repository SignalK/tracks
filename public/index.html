Import

A track of your own vessel is written into its recording, which is simplified once it is a day old: to half a metre, or to the tolerance the plugin's settings give, and not at all at 0. What simplifying leaves out cannot be brought back, so keep the GPX file if you want every point.

Import from history

A history provider holds your vessel's track. Importing it fills the time this plugin did not record, at the finest resolution the provider keeps. What was recorded stays as it is, and deleted parts stay deleted.

Delete the whole database

This deletes everything the plugin holds, and it cannot be undone:

  • the own vessel's whole recording,
  • every other vessel's track,
  • every imported track, and the recycle bin,
  • how far the history import got.

Keep a copy first: download the own vessel's whole recording as GPX, and export any imported track you want to keep from its row.

Parts of the own vessel's recording you deleted are forgotten too, so an import from a history provider afterwards brings them back.
